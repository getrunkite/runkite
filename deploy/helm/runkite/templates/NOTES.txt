Runkite control plane installed as {{ include "runkite.fullname" . }}.

1. Confirm pods are ready:
   kubectl get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}

2. Port-forward the API (ClusterIP Service by default):
   kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "runkite.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.httpPort }}:{{ .Values.service.httpPort }}
   curl -sS {{ include "runkite.httpScheme" . }}://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.service.httpPort }}/readyz

   RUNKITE_API_KEY entry in your existingSecret):
   curl -sS -H "Authorization: Bearer <api-key>" {{ include "runkite.httpScheme" . }}://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.service.httpPort }}/agents

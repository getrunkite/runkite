Scenario

Three agents. Two frameworks. One connector.

When one script stops being enough: coordinators and workers still need one place for credentials, HITL, kill, and audit.

Without a plane

Three scripts, three copies of the secret

Each process owns its env, HITL, and logs. Delegation is ad-hoc HTTP. Ops is SSH and grep.

With Runkite

One plane. Run-bound sessions. Shared Admin.

Clients speak Agent Protocol. Runners pull jobs. Connectors mint credentials from the assignment — not from forged headers.

On the plane

Governance that fails closed

Grants, HITL, fencing, and reclaim ceilings live on the plane — not as afterthought middleware in every script.

Connectors Minted per run. Workers never hold long-lived provider secrets.
HITL Approve or deny before a marked connector call proceeds.
Fencing A superseded runner cannot overwrite the winner after reclaim.
Poison pill Crash-loop jobs stop reclaiming after a hard ceiling.

Architecture

Clients never talk to runners

Agent Protocol on the front. Runner Protocol on the back. The plane owns lifecycle, policy, and proof. Pluggable state, transport, and vectors under an honest tier model — Supported for Postgres + Redis; Compatible for the rest.

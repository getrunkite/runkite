Why a control plane?

One script is fine until secrets, HITL, and logs multiply across agents and frameworks.

Use Runkite when
  • Several agents (or frameworks) need shared connectors, grants, HITL, kill, and audit.
  • You want clients on Agent Protocol and workers on a separate Runner Protocol.
  • You need fail-closed admission and fencing under reclaim — not best-effort glue.
Skip it when
  • A single local graph with its own env is enough.
  • You are still prototyping prompts on one laptop.
  • You need a managed cloud plane today (Runkite is self-host first).

Spend (FinOps)

Governance hooks for spend — not a chargeback dashboard product.

What the plane should own

  • Hard caps / deny on expensive connectors (policy next to grants).
  • Mandatory HITL before high-cost tool classes fire.
  • Durable audit of mint / approve / deny — exportable into your FinOps stack.

What v0.3 does not ship

  • No spend UI, budgets board, or chargeback product.
  • No automatic USD metering of every LLM token (bring your own exporter).

Practical stance

Keep cost controls on the plane beside grants and HITL. Ship audit to the system you already trust for money. Don’t wait on a Runkite “FinOps product.”

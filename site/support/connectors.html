Connectors & HITL

Fail-closed grants, durable audit, connector HITL — on the plane.

Connectors are plane-minted credentials for a run — workers never hold long-lived provider secrets. A grant must allow the agent + connector pair or the mint fails closed (no silent allow).

When a connector is marked for human-in-the-loop, the runner pauses and Admin (or the HITL API) must approve or deny before the call proceeds. Pending actions and grants live in Admin under Policy.

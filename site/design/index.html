Engineering notes

Reclaim, admission, and spend

How the control plane behaves when a runner is reclaimed, a stream starts before enqueue, serve would start insecure, a job crash-loops, concurrent runs share a budget, or Postgres RLS cannot grant itself. Failure modes and the code that closes them. Not a second architecture overview.

  • 1 · Generation fencing

    A stale runner cannot overwrite the winner after reclaim.

  • 2 · Subscribe-before-enqueue

    Listen on the event broker before dispatch so early tokens are not lost.

  • 3 · createRunCtx / fail-closed serve

    One admission funnel; production serve refuses an insecure posture.

  • 4 · Poison pill / reclaim ceiling

    Exhausted jobs stay recoverable until durable completion and finalization allow acknowledgement.

  • 5 · Optimistic spend holds

    Reserve an estimate at create time so concurrent runs can't all pass a terminal-only budget check at once.

  • 6 · The RLS grant that can't grant itself

    A Postgres 16 circular-grant gotcha that only shows up on a non-superuser DSN, and why superuser-only test coverage hid it.

  • 7 · Managed AI SDK receipts

    Commit native history before success; recover a committed turn without repeating tools.

  • 8 · Runtime hardening (v0.6.0, Markdown)

    Exact policy arguments, approval upgrades, fenced queue release, confirmed logout and bounded SDK/Admin delivery, with design rationale and regression evidence for contributors.

  • 9 · Atomic results and exact identities (v0.6.0, Markdown)

    Reuse completion transactions for cache hits, fence parent ownership, preserve HTTP identities and reject ambiguous native checkpoint keys.

  • 10 · Local HA recovery validation (v0.6.0, Markdown)

    Bounded multi-plane recovery cases, synthetic usage, retained streams and explicit limits on cloud HA claims.

  • 11 · Delegated authority and required audit (Markdown)

    Derive child authority from the parent, require durable decisions and keep provider diagnostics free of credentials.

  • 12 · Managed connector action evidence (unreleased, Markdown)

    Commit intent with approval consumption before a tool send. Preserve original run identity, correct retained checkpoint ordering and distinguish response observations from unknown business effects.

  • 13 · Tenant-scoped review (unreleased, Markdown)

    Give a tenant reviewer scoped approval or evidence access with separate session authority, without global Admin rights.

  • 14 · Target-bound approvals (unreleased, Markdown)

    Bind approval to the configured connector target and pin that snapshot through dispatch. Explain fresh review, secret-value exclusions, remote implementation limits and the SQL upgrade.

Integration identification artwork

These unmodified assets identify the projects named in the integration cards.
They do not indicate sponsorship, endorsement, adoption or a partnership.
Names and marks remain the property of their respective owners. Repository
software licenses do not grant general rights to those trademarks.

sources.json records each exact source, revision where available, asset hash
and original license location. Adjacent license files retain the upstream
repository notices. These files have their own terms; Runkite's Apache-2.0
license does not relicense third-party artwork.

promptfoo.svg: promptfoo's panda mark, MIT repository notice retained in
promptfoo-LICENSE.txt.

langflow.svg: Langflow's black icon, MIT repository notice retained in
langflow-LICENSE.txt.

langfuse.svg: Langfuse's application icon, repository license and scope retained
in langfuse-LICENSE.txt. The asset is outside the enterprise directories named
in that notice.

trinity.svg: Trinity's mobile application icon from abilityai/trinity.
The Apache-2.0 repository notice is retained in trinity-LICENSE.txt.

ag-ui.svg: AG-UI's documentation favicon, MIT repository notice retained in
ag-ui-LICENSE.txt.

ai-sdk.svg: the official AI SDK documentation favicon, depicting the Vercel
mark. It is not a newly created AI SDK logo. The upstream Apache-2.0 notice
is retained in ai-sdk-LICENSE.txt; the complete Apache-2.0 text also ships
in the repository's root LICENSE.

cline.svg: Cline's official white icon. The Apache-2.0 repository notice is
retained in cline-LICENSE.txt.

phoenix.svg: the Arize Phoenix icon distributed with its official plugin.
The repository's Elastic License 2.0 is retained in phoenix-LICENSE.txt.
Preserve the original color, proportions and clear space.
Brand guidance: https://github.com/Arize-ai/phoenix/tree/main/assets/brand

opentelemetry.svg: the OpenTelemetry color icon from cncf/artwork. Its artwork
terms are retained in opentelemetry-LICENSE.txt and refer to the Linux
Foundation trademark guidelines. This is not Apache-licensed artwork.
https://www.linuxfoundation.org/trademark-usage/

github.svg: the official white GitHub Invertocat, obtained from the archive
linked by https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo . It is trademark artwork,
not an Apache-licensed dependency. Use the supplied variant without recoloring,
effects or distortion, in a secondary role to identify the integration recipe.
The archive member and both archive and asset hashes are in sources.json.

The vLLM Semantic Router logo is reused from the parent assets directory.
Its original source and license are recorded in ../NOTICE.txt and
../vllm-semantic-router-LICENSE.txt.
